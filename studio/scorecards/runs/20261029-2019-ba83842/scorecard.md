### sodium-potassium-pump: the sodium-potassium pump (Anatomy and Physiology, high school)

Audience high school; 98 bpm. Core idea: Spending ATP, the pump pushes three sodium out and two potassium in, building gradients that power nerve signals.
Title line: "Pump sodium out, pull potassium in"

- **Hook 4.** The title line "Pump sodium out, pull potassium in" is concrete, singable, and opens and closes the chorus, but it names only the pump's action, not the big move of stored energy for nerve signals; i-2 "Charge up the brain before the signals begin" adds that. The lesson opens on a real question ("How can a nerve cell fire a signal the instant it's stimulated?") and the narration opens on the learner's world ("signals are racing through your brain"). All are solid, but the title line is one part of the idea.
- **Frame 4.** The water tower ("An electric pump fills a tank above the town") does the same job as the whole system: spend energy to store potential, then release it instantly. It is set up in v1-1 and recurs in the chorus ("stack the tower higher") and bridge ("the tower runs out"). The lesson and narration use it. It is dropped in the second verse and pre-choruses, and the lesson doesn't revisit it after the opening, so it is not fully carried through.
- **Why chain 5.** All five links are taught in order with causes: leak, then "Leaking is downhill; fixing it is uphill," then ATP changing shape, then the predict question about negative charge, then the sodium rush with no ATP. The lesson and narration explain each step and the mechanism in detail. The song carries the gist, though it spends a lot on the poison and heart drug and says little about why leaking happens or why the pump must spend energy.
- **Key lines 3.** In order, they tell the chain: leak, uphill needs ATP, 1 ATP for 3 out and 2 in, negative potential, sodium rush gives action potential. Key line 1 is a question rather than a step and sits on i-1, and line 2 "Electric pump = sodium-potassium pump; tank = sodium outside" is a label/mapping, not a step. The final line is about the ouabain poison, so the chain doesn't end on the core idea. Several lines use arrows and symbols, but they are short and plain.
- **Accuracy 4.** The core mechanism is correct: 3 Na+ out, 2 K+ in, one ATP, phosphorylation, resting potential about −70 mV, and no ATP for the sodium rush. The main imprecision is the chorus line "Inside turns negative" credited to the pump, though the lesson and narration correctly put the main cause on potassium leak. A teacher would tighten that and n28, but nothing a learner would carry away wrong.
- **Singability 3.** Most lines are whole spoken phrases with clean rhymes, and the chorus "Three out, two in, then go again" is chantable.
