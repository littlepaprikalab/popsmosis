labelled parts to the entry's subunits; you did not, and you judge it fresh.

(its title and organisms), and each labelled part with the subunits it was matched to, as the
entry names them, and, when the machine sits in a membrane, where the drawing puts it: above
the membrane, in it, or below it.

- `entry`: RIGHT when the entry is the machine the lesson teaches, from its organism or one
  close enough that the diagram teaches nothing false; else WRONG, with the problem in one
  sentence.
- For each part: RIGHT when those subunits are what the course means by that name, and only
  those; WRONG when they are another part, or the name needs subunits that are missing. Say
  the problem in one sentence, else "".
- `above` and `below`: for a machine in a membrane, the compartment on each side as the
  course calls it, from where the parts are ("STROMA" below when the catalytic knob is below
  the membrane), in capitals; "" for a machine in no membrane. Say "" for a side you cannot
  tell.
